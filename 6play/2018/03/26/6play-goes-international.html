The 6play platform goes international

Bedrock - March 26, 2018 - en

Within less than a year m6web and techm6web managed to launch websites, android apps and ios apps for three RTL TV channels in Europe, all based on the 6play technology.

These deployments include:

  • broadcasted and themathic channels,
  • live streaming, video catchup encoding and playout,
  • video resuming,
  • local adservers (for videos and display), DMP system and CDN,
  • contribution via our backoffice and automated through our API,
  • translations system,
  • and almost all the features of the 6play platform.

RTL Play (Belgium)

https://www.rtlplay.be/

RTL Play (Croatia)

https://play.rtl.hr/

RTL Most (Hungary)
