# Bedrock Tech Blog

The public engineering blog of Bedrock Streaming. It publishes written pieces by Bedrock engineers and keeps a public record of the talks they give, internally and at outside events.

## Language

### Content

**Article**:
A long-form written piece by one or more Bedrock engineers, meant to be read on the site.
_Avoid_: post, blog post, entry

**Talk**:
A presentation given by one or more Bedrock engineers at an Event, optionally with a Recording, Slides and a short write-up. A Talk is a record of something that happened, not something written to be read.
_Avoid_: video, conference, replay, session

**Event**:
The occasion at which a Talk was given — an outside conference or meetup, or Bedrock's own Last Friday Talks. Every Talk has exactly one Event.
_Avoid_: venue, conference

**Last Friday Talks**:
Bedrock's recurring internal talk series. It is an Event like any other, not a separate kind of content.
_Avoid_: LFT as a content type (LFT is fine as shorthand for the Event)

**Recording**:
A published video of a Talk.
_Avoid_: replay, video, youtubeId

**Slides**:
The published slide deck for a Talk.
_Avoid_: slideshare, deck

### Classification

**Topic**:
One of a small, closed, curated set of subject areas that an Article or Talk belongs to. A Topic exists to be browsed; a subject nobody browses is not a Topic.
_Avoid_: tag, category, keyword, label

**Language**:
The natural language an Article or Talk is written or delivered in. French and English both occur; every piece of content declares one.
_Avoid_: locale, lang, i18n

### People

**Author**:
The Bedrock engineer, Bedrock team, or Bedrock itself credited with writing an Article.
_Avoid_: contributor, writer

**Speaker**:
The Bedrock engineer who gave a Talk.
_Avoid_: presenter, author (an Article has Authors, a Talk has Speakers)
